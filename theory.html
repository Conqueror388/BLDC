PHYSICS REFERENCE // 3-PHASE ARMATURE & FIELD

Electromagnetics & Working Principle

A Brushless DC motor operates on Lorentz force interactions: F = I · (L × B). Solid-state MOSFET inverters replace physical carbon commutators, switching current through copper phase pairs to keep the stator magnetic vector orthogonal to the permanent magnet rotor.

[01 // STATOR ARMATURE] STATIONARY

Silicon Steel Laminations & 3-Phase Windings

The stator core uses thin, insulated silicon-steel laminations (0.2mm to 0.35mm thickness) to suppress circulating eddy currents and reduce hysteresis loss:

  • STAR (WYE) CONFIG: Windings share a central neutral point. Line-to-line voltage is V_LL = √3 · V_phase, phase current equals line current (I_phase = I_line). Yields higher starting torque (Kt_wye = √3 · Kt_delta) and an accessible virtual neutral for sensorless zero-crossing detection.
  • DELTA (Δ) CONFIG: Closed loop with no neutral wire. Line voltage equals phase voltage (V_line = V_phase) and line current is I_line = √3 · I_phase. Yields 1.732× higher rotational speed (Kv_delta = √3 · Kv_wye) for the same terminal voltage, popular in high-speed racing drones.
  • Each phase coil pair (A, B, C) is spaced geometrically 120° apart on the stator iron core.
[02 // ROTOR FIELD] ROTATING

Permanent Magnets & Pole Topologies

High-energy Neodymium-Iron-Boron (NdFeB, Grade N48–N52) rare-earth magnets form alternating North and South magnetic poles on the spinning element:

  • OUTRUNNER (BELL): Permanent magnets mounted on the outer rotating bell surrounding the inner stationary stator. Provides high torque constant (Kt) for direct-drive propellers and hub motors.
  • INRUNNER: Cylindrical rotor rotates inside an outer stator housing. Low rotational inertia permits extreme speeds exceeding 50,000 RPM (RC cars, EDF fans).
  • ELECTRICAL FREQUENCY EQUATION: f_elec = (P_poles / 2) × (RPM / 60). For a 14-pole motor at 12,000 RPM: f_elec = 7 × 200 = 1,400 Hz.

Six-Step 120° Trapezoidal Commutation Matrix

60° ELECTRICAL SECTORS

During each 60° electrical sector, exactly two phase windings conduct current (one tied to V_bus high, one tied to GND low) while the third phase remains floating (High-Z). The magnetic vector generated by the stator maintains an angular displacement of 60° to 120° ahead of the rotor flux, exerting continuous forward torque.

[03 // HALL SENSORS] 0 RPM START

Digital Hall Position Decoding

Three bipolar latching Hall effect ICs are installed 120° apart on the stator periphery. As rotor poles pass:

  • NORTH POLE FLUX: Pulls sensor output logic 1.
  • SOUTH POLE FLUX: Resets sensor output logic 0.
  • Forms six 3-bit binary words (101, 100, 110, 010, 011, 001). The invalid states 000 and 111 immediately trigger hardware fault protection.
  • Delivers full starting torque from 0 RPM (electric scooters, robotic servos, gimbals).
[04 // BACK-EMF] HIGH RELIABILITY

Sensorless Zero-Crossing Detection

Eliminates sensor wires in severe environments. The ESC measures the induced Back-EMF on the unpowered floating phase winding:

  • Induced voltage follows generator principle: V_bemf = Ke · ω.
  • When this voltage passes through the virtual center tap (V_bus / 2), a comparator registers a Zero-Crossing Event.
  • Microcontroller timer delays 30° electrical degrees, then executes commutation.
  • At 0 RPM, V_bemf = 0, requiring an open-loop stepper-style ramp-up sequence.
